## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an institutional, forward-looking visual identity engineered for Indonesian primary, secondary, and vocational educational institutions. It bridges two distinct operational surfaces: a welcoming, credible public school profile designed to inspire confidence in prospective parents and students, and an efficient, clutter-free Content Management System (CMS) backend for administrative staff, educators, and curriculum leads.

The aesthetic fuses **Corporate / Modern** structure with **Academic Rigor**, balancing ceremonial dignity and technological agility:
- **Trust & Prestige:** Institutional deep navy reflects longevity, stability, and governmental alignment with national education standards (Kemendikbudristek).
- **Academic Distinction:** Strategic warm amber accents highlight honors, akreditasi grades, and student achievement without feeling gaudy or promotional.
- **Approachable Clarity:** High whitespace ratios, open typography, and deliberate contrast eliminate bureaucratic density, making administrative tasks swift and public discovery frictionless.

## Colors

The color palette establishes an authoritative hierarchy rooted in oceanic deep blues and crisp surfaces:

- **Primary (`#1E3A8A`):** Deep Trust Navy. Serves as the bedrock of the design system. Applied to primary CTAs, institutional headers, major headings, key brand graphics, active sidebar states, and formal certificates.
- **Secondary (`#2563EB`):** Royal Azure. Used for interactive hyperlinks, active navigation highlights, dynamic focal points, secondary system buttons, and current-state tabs.
- **Tertiary (`#D97706` / `#F59E0B`):** Academic Gold / Warm Amber. Expresses institutional excellence, accreditation honors (Akreditasi A / Unggul), scholarship callouts, badges of honor, and achievement metrics.
- **Neutral Palette:** 
  - Slate Dark (`#0F172A` / `#1E293B`): Primary copy, high-contrast table headers, and structural icons.
  - Slate Mid (`#64748B`): Secondary text, timestamps, captions, breadcrumbs, and placeholder labels.
  - Ice Blue & Slate Surfaces (`#F8FAFC`, `#F1F5F9`): Main canvas canvas fill, nested administrative panels, table alternations, and CMS sidebars.
  - Pristine White (`#FFFFFF`): Elevated card containers, modal dialogues, input fields, and public content blocks.
- **System Feedback:** Success (`#059669`), Warning (`#D97706`), Destructive/Alert (`#DC2626`), and Information (`#0284C7`).

## Typography

The design system relies on **Plus Jakarta Sans** across all roles to unify institutional authority with contemporary digital legibility. Designed with geometric clarity and balanced proportions, it remains crisp within complex data matrices and prominent on public landing banners.

- **Display & Headlines:** Set in weights `700` and `800` with subtle negative tracking (`-0.02em` on Display, `-0.01em` on Headlines). Used for landing hero titles, institutional mottos, and major CMS dashboard milestones.
- **Body:** Standardized at `15px` for default copy (`body-md`) to ensure comfortable scanning of curriculum guides, news articles, and administrative guidelines.
- **Labels & Badges:** Rendered with `weight: 600` or `700` to deliver punchy, legible categorization (e.g., "PENGUMUMAN", "PRESTASI", "PPDB ONLINE").
- **Multilingual Support:** Optimally proportioned for formal Indonesian (Bahasa Indonesia baku) and English international curriculum copy.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout architecture employs a dual-framework rhythm based on a **4px/8px modular scale**:

### 1. Public Profile Framework
- **Container Max-Width:** 1280px for standard content; 1440px for full-width interactive showcases.
- **Grid Structure:** 12-column responsive fluid grid on desktop (`gutter: 1.5rem`, `margin: 2rem`), adapting to 8 columns on tablet and 4 columns on mobile screens (`gutter: 1rem`, `margin: 1rem`).
- **Section Spacing:** Generous structural breathing room (`space-xl` scaled up to 5rem between public sections) to promote readability and clear division between announcements, programs, and testimonials.

### 2. CMS Backend Architecture
- **Admin Workspace:** Fixed-width 260px collapsible sidebar combined with a fluid main dashboard panel.
- **Data Densities:** Compact component padding (`space-xs` to `space-sm` for inline controls; `space-md` for tabular rows) that optimizes screen real estate for student lists, grades, and article management.
- **Breakpoints:**
  - `sm`: 640px (single column stack, off-canvas drawers)
  - `md`: 768px (sidebar collapse to icon rail, multi-card wrap)
  - `lg`: 1024px (full dual-column dashboard, sticky navigation)
  - `xl`: 1280px (optimized desktop layout)

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids heavy, dark drop-shadows in favor of **ambient, tinted depth tiers** using subtle navy-slate diffusion (`rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)` to `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08)`). Surfaces feel floating, clean, and structurally grounded.

- **Level 0 (Flat / Canvas):** Neutral background fills (`#F8FAFC` for CMS, `#FFFFFF` for primary web content). No shadow.
- **Level 1 (Subtle Surface Card):** Card components, academic profile modules, and table shells.
  - Shadow: `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05)`.
  - Border: 1px solid `#E2E8F0`.
- **Level 2 (Interactive & Hover):** Active cards on cursor focus, quick-action tiles, and active filters.
  - Shadow: `0 4px 6px -1px rgba(30, 58, 138, 0.06), 0 2px 4px -2px rgba(30, 58, 138, 0.06)`.
  - Border: 1px solid `#CBD5E1`.
- **Level 3 (Overlays & Menus):** Navigation dropdowns, administrative contextual menus, popovers, and sticky subheaders.
  - Shadow: `0 10px 15px -3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 4px 6px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
- **Level 4 (Modals & Drawers):** Student profile dialogs, media uploaders, and announcement composers.
  - Shadow: `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.1), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
  - Backdrop: `#0F172A` with 40% opacity and 4px blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(4px)`).

## Shapes

The design system incorporates **Roundedness Level 2** (`0.5rem` / `8px` default radius), projecting contemporary digital precision while maintaining friendly institutional warmth:

- **Base Corner Radius (`8px` / `0.5rem`):** Applied universally to data cards, standard buttons, text input fields, modal containers, and metric stat blocks.
- **Nested Inner Components (`6px` / `0.375rem`):** Form dropdowns, child tiles, and table row selections nested within parent cards.
- **Large Sections (`16px` / `1rem` - `24px` / `1.5rem`):** Feature banners, hero showcase media wrappers, and public marketing blocks.
- **Pill Geometry (`9999px`):** Reserved exclusively for status indicators, announcement badges, category tags, user profile avatars, and icon action pills.

## Components

### 1. Header & Navigation Ecosystem
- **Top Utility Info-Bar:** Sits at the absolute top of the public site in Navy Blue (`#1E3A8A`) with white/slate-200 micro text. Displays operational hours, NPSN/Akreditasi indicators, emergency contact, and direct links to "Portal Guru", "PPDB Online", and "CMS Login".
- **Primary Sticky Navbar:** White surface with subtle blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(8px); background: rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.95)`). Includes the official school crest, primary menu links with bottom border indicator on active state (`#2563EB`), and a distinct gold/amber accent CTA for "Daftar Sekarang / PPDB".

### 2. Buttons
- **Primary:** Background `#1E3A8A`, text `#FFFFFF`, radius `8px`, font weight `600`. Subtle hover shift to `#2563EB`. Active state scales to `0.98`.
- **Secondary:** Background `#F1F5F9`, text `#1E3A8A`, border `1px solid #CBD5E1`. Hover changes background to `#E2E8F0`.
- **Achievement / Accent CTA:** Background `#D97706`, text `#FFFFFF`, focus ring `#F59E0B`. Used for high-conversion enrollment links and honors.
- **Ghost / Tertiary:** Transparent background, text `#2563EB`, hover background `#F8FAFC`.

### 3. Pill Badges & Chips
- Fully rounded (`rounded-full`), height `24px` to `28px`, text size `12px` (`label-md`).
- **Category Badge (Public):** Background `rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.08)`, text `#2563EB`.
- **Achievement / Akreditasi Badge:** Background `rgba(217, 119, 6, 0.1)`, text `#B45309`, left-aligned mini trophy or star icon.
- **Status Pills (CMS):**
  - "Published / Aktif": Background `#DCFCE7`, text `#15803D`.
  - "Draft / Diperiksa": Background `#FEF3C7`, text `#B45309`.
  - "Archived / Nonaktif": Background `#F1F5F9`, text `#64748B`.

### 4. Cards & Metric Tiles
- **School Profile Elevated Cards:** `#FFFFFF` background, `1px solid #E2E8F0`, subtle shadow Level 1. Upper thumbnail with 16:9 aspect ratio, content area with `space-md` padding, and bottom metadata row separated by a hairline border.
- **CMS Stat Cards:** Feature a compact white container, mini circular icon badge (Navy, Blue, or Gold background tint), large number metric (`headline-md`), and a subtle trend indicator (e.g., `+12% thn ini` with green arrow icon).

### 5. Data Tables (CMS Core)
- **Header:** Background `#F8FAFC`, uppercase `11px` bold text (`label-sm`), color `#64748B`, tracking `0.05em`.
- **Rows:** Alternating subtle row hover (`#F8FAFC`), vertical padding `12px`, border-bottom `1px solid #F1F5F9`.
- **Actions Cell:** Fixed right alignment with ghost icon buttons (Edit, Preview, Archive) with smooth color transitions.

### 6. Administrative Sidebar
- Background `#0F172A` (or pure `#FFFFFF` light-mode alternative depending on admin preference, defaulting to crisp `#FFFFFF` with `#F1F5F9` active tracks).
- Nav items: 40px height, `8px` corner radius, left icon in `#64748B`, text transitioning to `#1E3A8A` with an active indicator badge on the far right.

### 7. Form Controls & Inputs
- Text inputs and dropdowns feature a `1px solid #CBD5E1` border on `#FFFFFF` background, `8px` corner radius, and `14px` typography.
- Focus state triggers a clean `2px solid #2563EB` border with a diffused `0 0 0 3px rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.12)` halo. Checkboxes and radio buttons adhere to the primary navy tone when checked.
